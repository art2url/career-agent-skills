# Executive Leadership Resume

## Trigger

Use when the user is applying for C-suite, VP, or Director roles with 15+ years of senior leadership experience.

Keywords: "executive resume", "leadership resume", "C-suite resume", "CEO resume", "CTO resume", "CFO resume", "VP resume", "director resume", "senior leadership", "board resume", "executive search", "executive CV", "senior executive", "managing director resume", "chief officer resume"

## Key Differences

- **2-3 pages** acceptable (not 1)
- **Strategic impact** over tactical execution
- **Transformation stories** over task lists
- **Leadership brand** over skills inventory
- Audience: executive recruiters, board members, PE/VC, CHROs

## Structure

1. Executive Profile (replaces summary)
2. Core Competencies (leadership themes, not a skills list)
3. Career Highlights (4-6 top achievements upfront)
4. Professional Experience
5. Board & Advisory Roles
6. Education & Executive Development

## Process

### Step 1: Executive Profile

```
[FUNCTION] EXECUTIVE | [DOMAIN 1] | [DOMAIN 2]

[Leadership brand — 1-2 sentences on who you are as a leader]
[Track record with scope: revenue, team size, geographies, exits]
[What you're known for / unique value prop]
```

### Step 2: Core Competencies

Frame as leadership themes, not skills:
```
Strategic Growth        | M&A Integration        | Digital Transformation
P&L Management ($X+)   | Global Team Leadership  | Board Relations
```

### Step 3: Career Highlights

4-6 biggest achievements before chronological experience. Each must have a metric at organizational scale: revenue growth, margin improvement, exits, headcount scaled, transformation outcomes.

### Step 4: Experience with Strategic Framing

For each role include:
- **Company context**: revenue, headcount, stage, industry
- **Your mandate**: why you were hired or promoted
- **Reporting structure**: span of control, direct reports
- **4-6 strategic initiatives with outcomes**

Bullet formula: **[Leadership Action] + [Strategic Initiative] + [Business Outcome at Scale]**

Example: "Led operational transformation improving EBITDA margins from 12% to 28% while growing revenue from $150M to $400M over 3 years"

### Step 5: Board & Advisory

Format: Role | Organization | Dates | Context (revenue, committee roles)

## Tone

Use: Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Championed, Transformed, Architected, Steered.

Avoid: Managed, Handled, Assisted (too tactical for executives).

## Output Format

```
## Executive Resume

### Executive Profile
[Full profile]

### Core Competencies
[Theme grid]

### Career Highlights
[4-6 achievements]

### Experience
[Strategic role writeups]

### Positioning Notes
- Narrative theme: [story your resume tells]
- Leadership brand: [what you're known for]
- Differentiator: [what sets you apart]
```

## Rules

- Show what changed because of you — executives are hired to transform
- Always include company context so achievements have scale
- Use percentages when absolute numbers are confidential
- Frame short tenures around missions ("Recruited for turnaround; completed in 18 months")
- Everything will be fact-checked — keep it defensible
