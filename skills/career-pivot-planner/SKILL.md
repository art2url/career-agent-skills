# Career Pivot Planner

## Trigger

Use when the user is switching careers or industries and needs a plan for translating their experience.

Keywords: "career pivot", "career pivot plan", "career change", "career transition", "switching careers", "changing industries", "new industry", "new career", "transferable skills", "transition plan", "career switch", "mid-career change", "leaving teaching", "military to civilian", "industry change", "reskilling", "how to change careers"

## Process

### Step 1: Map Transferable Skills

Identify universal skills from current experience:
- **Leadership**: team management, project oversight, mentoring, decision-making
- **Communication**: presentations, writing, client relations, negotiation
- **Analytical**: data analysis, problem-solving, process improvement, research
- **Operational**: process design, vendor management, training, quality assurance

Map each to target industry's language.

### Step 2: Translate Terminology

Create a translation table from current to target industry. Common patterns:

- **Teaching → Corporate**: students → learners, lesson plans → training curriculum, assessments → evaluations, classroom management → group facilitation
- **Military → Corporate**: mission → project, command → leadership, briefing → presentation, deployment → implementation
- **Retail → B2B**: customers → clients, store sales → revenue generation, upselling → cross-selling
- **Healthcare → Tech**: patient care → client service, EMR → CRM, rounds → status meetings, protocols → SOPs

### Step 3: Reframe Experience

Rewrite bullets using target industry language while keeping achievements truthful.

**Before** (teacher): "Taught 25 students in 5th grade classroom"
**After** (L&D): "Designed and delivered curriculum for 25 learners, achieving 95% proficiency through data-driven instruction"

### Step 4: Build Transition Narrative

Draft a "why" story:
1. **Discovery**: "Through [experience], I found my interest in [new field]..."
2. **Connection**: "My background in [old field] gives me unique perspective because..."
3. **Action**: "To prepare, I've [certifications, projects, volunteering]..."
4. **Vision**: "I'm excited to bring [transferable skill] to [target company]..."

Must be positive and forward-looking — never "I'm burned out" or "I want more money."

### Step 5: Recommend Bridge Experiences

Suggest immediate credibility builders:
- Relevant certifications (specific to target field)
- Volunteer work in target industry
- Freelance or consulting projects
- Side projects with demonstrable outcomes
- Online courses with portfolio-worthy outputs

### Step 6: Recommend Resume Structure

For career changers, use hybrid format:
1. Summary — targeted at new role, explaining the pivot
2. Core Competencies — transferable skills in target language
3. Relevant Experience — grouped by skill, not chronology
4. Additional Experience — chronological, brief
5. Education & Certifications — including bridge credentials

## Rules

- Always reframe, never fabricate — real experience is the foundation
- Use target industry language throughout
- Recommend bridge experiences that can be started immediately
- Lead with transferable skills, not job titles
